A diagram session can be read-only (readOnly, setReadOnly): the user pans, zooms and selects, and nothing edits the drawing. A host drives its view from outside: showSelection and showHover set what is drawn selected and hovered, and onSelect, onHover and onActivate report the user's selection, hover and double click or Enter, each with the object's kind.
